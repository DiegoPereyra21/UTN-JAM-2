AUTHORSHIP AND PROVENANCE

All geometry and all textures are original work created for this package.

  * No third-party models, scans, photographs or textures were used.
  * The textures are bakes of procedural Blender shaders. No photographic
    source material and no image generators were involved.
  * The geometry is produced deterministically by the two Python generators
    included in src/. Nothing was sampled from a 3D generative model.

No real-world logos, trademarks or protected designs are depicted. The gates
and fence are generic constructions, not reproductions of any manufacturer's
product.

AI DISCLOSURE

The generator code was written in an agent-assisted workflow: an AI assistant
(Claude) authored the Python that builds the geometry, driving Blender through
an MCP bridge. The output is code-authored geometry, not generated geometry -
but the distinction matters to some platforms, so it is stated plainly here
rather than buried.
